# Privacy Policy

_Effective Date: April 14, 2025_

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how we handle and protect your personal information in relation to your use of our Chrome extension. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your data.

## Information Collection

Our Chrome Extension does not collect or transmit any personal information from users. Specifically:

- We do not gather any personal data such as names, email addresses, or location information
- We do not transmit any data to external servers
- We do not track user behavior or browsing history
- We do not use cookies or similar tracking technologies

The extension does use local storage to store certain non-personal data locally on your device:
- Alchemy Account Kit configuration and settings are stored in your browser's local storage
- This data remains exclusively on your device and is never transmitted externally
- You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser's local storage
- Since Alchemy Account Kit is a third-party service, we recommend reviewing their privacy policy for any additional information about their data handling practices

## Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit any personal information, there is no risk of data breaches or unauthorized access to user data. Your privacy is protected by design. Any data stored locally in your browser's storage remains secure and private to your device.

## Third-Party Services

Our extension integrates with third-party SDKs and services. This Privacy Policy does not cover how these third parties process personal information. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services whose SDKs we integrate with, particularly:

- Alchemy Account Kit SDK

## Changes to This Privacy Policy
